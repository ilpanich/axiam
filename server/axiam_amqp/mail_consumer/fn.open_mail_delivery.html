Admit one delivery taken off axiam.mail.outbound: verify its signed envelope through gate and decode the OutboundMailMessage inside.

Returns None — the caller nacks without requeue, which dead-letters it — for an unsigned, tampered, stale or replayed message, or a body that is not a mail message, after appending an amqp.internal_message_rejected row to the system audit log. Broker-free so it can be tested directly.

redelivered is the broker’s flag on the delivery: a redelivery of a mail already admitted (this consumer died before it acked) is admitted again rather than dead-lettered as a replay (SR1-1).
