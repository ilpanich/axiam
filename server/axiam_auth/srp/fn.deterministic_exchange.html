Compute a complete exchange from fixed inputs.

Deterministic by construction — a and b are parameters rather than random — which is what makes the output usable as a committed test vector. Both the client-side and server-side derivations of S are computed and asserted equal, so a vector can never be published that the two halves of the protocol disagree about.

§Panics

If the two derivations of S disagree, which would mean this module’s own client and server halves are inconsistent — a bug that must never reach a published vector.
