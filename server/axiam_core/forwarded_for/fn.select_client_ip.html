The address a per-address rate limit keys a request on, and the one audit rows and log fields record (SEC-048, T-212, #711, #752).

peer, the transport peer, when trusted_proxies does not honour its header. Otherwise the rightmost untrusted X-Forwarded-For hop — hops[len - 1 - trusted_hops] — when the header has more hops than trusted_hops and that entry parses as an address; otherwise peer. A header too short to reach past the trusted hops is ignored entirely rather than indexed from the left (SECHRD-03).
