Turn a delete transaction’s statement errors into the right DbError (SEC-104).

Scans every statement’s error rather than trusting Response::check(), for the reason resource::delete already documents: a THROW fires on its own slot while the trailing statements report the generic “not executed due to a failed transaction”, and check() can surface one of those instead — which would silently downgrade a precise 404 into an opaque 500.

extra lets a repository claim a THROW of its own (resource’s child-count guard) before the generic mapping runs.
