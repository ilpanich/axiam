The bounded wait SAML signature verification gives the deployment’s crypto semaphore (#654). The bounded wait for the deployment’s crypto semaphore (#654, R101-02).

Every SAML signature verification (the ACS’s, the metadata’s, the IdP receiver’s) runs on the blocking pool under the one semaphore that also bounds Argon2id and the CRL re-sign. The semaphore is fair, so an acquire with no bound queues behind every earlier waiter, and the SAML routes are unauthenticated: a flood of signed posts would hold the bounded waiters (logins, a CRL re-sign) at their own 503 for as long as it lasted. A SAML acquire therefore gives up after the same hash_acquire_timeout_secs the Argon2id path uses, and the caller answers with the refusal its path already has.

The permit is taken before anything about the document is evaluated, so a busy gate says nothing about a signature: an invalid signature and a busy gate can be answered alike without an oracle.
