Expand description

Verify a POST-binding request’s enveloped signature against the SP’s registered certificate (DER). Call only after parse_authn_request answered enveloped_signature: true, which is what established that the one signature in the document is the root’s own.

§Errors

RequestError::SignatureInvalid — a wrong key, a changed byte, a SHA-1 algorithm, or anything xmlsec refuses.
