The grants a self-registered client may hold.

The two halves of a browser-driven code flow and nothing else. client_credentials and the RFC 8693 exchange are absent for the reason GRANTS_FORBIDDEN_TO_PUBLIC_CLIENTS gives in the admin handler and one more besides: both mint a token on the strength of the client’s own identity, and the identity of a self-registered client is a row a stranger created. The device grant is absent because a device that cannot open a browser cannot run a registration request either.
