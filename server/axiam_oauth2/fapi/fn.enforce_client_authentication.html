Request-time check of the profile’s client-authentication rule alone (FAPI 2.0 §5.3.1.1; D-17, T23.1.5).

A fapi2 client whose row names a shared-secret method — which validate_registration can never have admitted, so the row was edited past it — is refused invalid_client, with the same error and the same error! at every endpoint that authenticates a client. A no-op for a standard client.

This is the rule enforce_token_request applies first, extracted rather than copied so the endpoints cannot drift. It is the whole of the gate at PAR, introspection and revocation, because the rest of enforce_token_request asks for evidence those endpoints do not establish before a client authenticates (a DPoP proof is verified after authentication at PAR and not at all at the other two), and a legitimate DPoP-bound client would be refused by it. Call it after the client has authenticated: the refusal then reveals a fact about the row only to a caller holding the row’s credential.
