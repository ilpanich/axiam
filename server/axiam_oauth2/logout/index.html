OIDC RP-Initiated Logout 1.0 and Back-Channel Logout 1.0 — B5.

§Why the two exist together

A federation deployment is AXIAM plus N relying parties. Each half closes a hole the other leaves:

  • Without RP-initiated logout, a user who logs out of an RP stays logged in at AXIAM, so the next “Login with AXIAM” silently re-authenticates them. From the user’s point of view they did not log out.
  • Without back-channel logout, a user who logs out of AXIAM stays logged in at every RP, indefinitely, because nothing tells the RPs. This is the one that matters: an admin revoking a compromised account leaves N live sessions behind.

§Session precision

Both halves operate on a session, not a user. A user with a phone and a laptop who logs out on the laptop expects the phone to stay signed in, and RP-Initiated Logout 1.0 §2 agrees. That is why the ID token carries sid and why the logout token always names it.
