The namespace of AXIAM’s assurance-level-change levels (CAEP 1.0 §3.4 allows a transmitter-defined alias). The levels are the two acr values AXIAM publishes, crate::oidc::ACR_SINGLE_FACTOR and crate::oidc::ACR_MULTI_FACTOR (D-45).
