The retry policy of the Vault revocation sweep (T-470, #603).

A revocation Vault refuses is not retried at every tick: each refusal pushes the row’s next attempt out (1 minute, doubling, to 6 hours), a replica claims a row before it attempts it, and a row that keeps failing past FAILING_AFTER_ATTEMPTS is reported as its own job-health error. The constants and the two pure functions live here, apart from the sweep, so the next consumer of the same bookkeeping (a CA certificate’s revocation) does not copy them.

Constants§

BACKOFF_BASE
The wait after a row’s first refusal.
BACKOFF_CAP
The longest wait between two attempts at one row.
CLAIM_LEASE
How long a replica holds a claimed row. Longer than one sweep pass (crate::cert bounds it to 30 seconds), so a row being attempted is not due for anyone else; a replica that dies mid-attempt loses the row for this long and no longer, with nothing counted against the row.
FAILING_AFTER_ATTEMPTS
Refusals after which a row counts as failing for good, and /health/jobs reports it (as vault_revocation_failing) instead of failing the pass that retries it. Twelve attempts span about a day: the first nine back off from one minute to a little over four hours, the rest wait the six-hour cap.
MAX_REASON_CHARS
The longest reason a row keeps, in characters: a status and a short message.

Functions§

backoff
How long to wait before the next attempt at a row that has now failed attempts times (1 for the first refusal): BACKOFF_BASE doubled for each earlier refusal, capped at BACKOFF_CAP.

A custodian’s error as a row may keep it: control characters and runs of whitespace collapsed, anything that reads like a Vault token replaced, and the whole cut to MAX_REASON_CHARS.
