The stop for SIGTERM and for a dead consumer or gRPC server: REST 20 s, gRPC 5, lease release 1, outbound 2, audit 5, cleanup 5 — 38 s, inside the shipped 40 s grace period with a 2 s margin that [FATAL_STOP_BACKSTOP] sits in. The lease release (#628) was unbounded until 1.1.0 and outside this sum; its 1 s came out of the margin, which was 3 s.
