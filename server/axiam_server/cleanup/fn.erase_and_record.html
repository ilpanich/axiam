Expand description

An erasure through its point of no return to its record (#624): the steps of run_erasure_pipeline_reporting in its order — audit scrub, anonymization, SSF report, proof — then the #668 second revocation, the gdpr.user_pseudonymized audit row, and the account_deletion row marked completed (#689 (i): the record first). Run inside a unit of AuditedWork, so a cut of the cleanup task does not reach it.

The anonymization is the point of no return: it clears deletion_pending, so no later sweep selects the account again, and in the same transaction sets the account’s record marker (#689) to a stamp this call chose (#737). The marker’s guard is armed from that stamp before the write — the orphan unit’s pattern since #714 — so the marker is guarded from the instant it exists: cleared with the completion once the audit row is written, and released on every other exit, awaited where it can be and by the guard’s drop on a cut, so the next sweep (record_owed_erasures) writes at once what the erasure still owes: the proof, the second revocation and the record. A release whose anonymization never landed changes nothing, since it is conditional on the stamp. Until the anonymization — the audit scrub — the call gives up once work is stopping and answers Ok(false): the account stays due. Past it, it runs on to the audit row whatever the stop says, and answers Ok(true). A free function, and public, for the reason run_erasure_pipeline is one.

Ok(false) too, with nothing written, when another unit erased the account after taking this one’s claim over past its lease (#738, #784): the account is read again before the scrub and left alone once it is no longer deletion-pending, and the anonymization writes only a deletion-pending account, so a unit that stalls after that read stops at the anonymization, before the SSF report — its scrub having passed over the other unit’s record, which the scrub never touches. And Ok(false) when the erasure proof turns out to exist already: the next sweep wrote it for this unit, stalled past the record lease after its anonymization; this call records nothing, and releases its marker, which that sweep settles.

§Errors

Those of the account’s read, the scrub and the anonymization, before anything is recorded; and a proof that failed to write, past the point of no return, with the marker released so that the next sweep writes the proof and the record. The completion and the audit row are not fallible here: a failed completion is logged, and a failed audit write is dead-lettered (SECHRD-12).
