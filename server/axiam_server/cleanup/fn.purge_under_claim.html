Expand description

One due account’s erasure under an exclusive claim (#738): claim it at the claimant’s own now, run erasure — the unit’s steps from the first destructive one to the record — and release the claim unless the account was erased.

Ok(false), with nothing run, when the account is no longer due (its deletion was cancelled since the sweep’s read, or another unit erased it) or another unit holds a claim on it younger than lease: two sweeps that read one due account erase it once, and the one that skips it reports no error. Otherwise the outcome is erasure’s. The claim is released, awaited, on every outcome but Ok(true), and by its guard’s drop if this future is dropped — a unit cut at the teardown’s bound, or a panic — so the next sweep takes the account at once; the release changes nothing once the anonymization has run.

A free function, and public, for the reason run_erasure_pipeline is: axiam-server‘s integration tests run two sweeps’ units against one store.

§Errors

A datastore failure of the claim (nothing ran), or erasure’s.
