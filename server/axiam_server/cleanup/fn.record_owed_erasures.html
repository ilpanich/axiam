Expand description

Finish the erasures whose record is still owed — a unit cut between the anonymization and the clearing of its marker (#689) — and settle each: the first step of every GDPR purge sweep; none starts once the process is stopping.

SurrealAccountDeletionRepository::claim_owed_erasure_records claims each owed erasure, conditionally on the marker read: a marker released by a cut unit or older than lease, and (#689 (i)) an anonymized account whose deletion request is still pending with no marker at all — an erasure cut before schema v108 between its anonymization and its completion. For each, it redoes the part of the erasure’s tail the unit may not have reached (#737), in the unit’s order:

  1. the erasure proof, unless the account has one: the proof’s unique index refuses a second, which reads as “written already” — the unit’s own, or a racing sweep’s. It carries the pseudonym on the row and is dated from the marker the claim read, or now when the marker was released or unset;
  2. the #668 second revocation, by binding only (empty names), which is idempotent: a certificate bound to the account between the unit’s (b4) and its anonymization is revoked even when the unit was cut before its own pass;
  3. the gdpr.user_pseudonymized row, unless the tenant’s trail already holds one for the account (a unit cut after its record and before the clearing), matched by its actor or by its pseudonym — before 1.1.2 (#784) a unit that stalled past its claim’s lease ran its audit scrub over the row another unit wrote and nilled its actor, which the scrub no longer does: an account is erased once, so that row is this erasure’s. It carries the pseudonym the anonymization wrote, certificates_revoked as null — the unit’s count lived only in the cut unit, and the second pass’s alone would understate it — and recovered: true.

Then the marker is cleared and the request completed, in one transaction. The claim is held by the same guard the unit holds. A failure of step 1 or 2 releases the claim, so the next sweep retries the account whole: no request is completed without its proof, nor before the second revocation ran.

Ok(n) is the number of records this call wrote.

§Errors

The datastore error that kept the owed erasures from being read or claimed, or AxiamError::Internal naming how many could not be finished (each of those is released for the next sweep).
