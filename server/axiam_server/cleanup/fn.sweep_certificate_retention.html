Expand description

The certificate_retention job (R101W1-05, #618): delete certificate rows of live tenants more than eight days past their notAfter, in bounded batches, and return how many.

A revoked row is kept for as long as its issuer’s revocation list can name it (the tenant purge’s KEPT_REVOCATION, whose grace this sweep shares); past it the CRL is the same without the row. Nothing deleted a certificate row in a live tenant before, so a revoked one — and the PEM, subject and metadata in it, of an account the Art. 17 purge had erased — outlived any purpose for the tenant’s lifetime. A deleted tenant’s rows are the tenant purge’s, not this sweep’s. Two replicas may run it at once: each batch is chosen and deleted by id, and a row the other replica removed first is not counted. Touches no audit row.

Runs at most CERTIFICATE_RETENTION_MAX_BATCHES batches of CERTIFICATE_RETENTION_BATCH rows, and stops between batches on a shutdown signal; the next tick continues. A free function, and public, for the reason run_erasure_pipeline is.

§Errors

The repository’s error from the batch that failed; batches already deleted stay deleted.
