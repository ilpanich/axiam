One pass of the tenant_purge job: purge every tombstoned tenant and, when scan_orphans, every orphaned tenant id.

A free function, and public, for the reason run_erasure_pipeline is one. Each tenant is purged by SurrealTenantRepository::purge_tombstoned_rows — every tenant-scoped table in the order user erasure uses, of its audit trail the rows at or before the time of the export the deletion required (T-118, R1W1-04) — and then SurrealTenantRepository::remove_purged_tenant, its tenant row; or, for an orphan, under its claim marker, by SurrealTenantRepository::purge_orphan_rows, which leaves the audit trail to the retention sweep, and then by the same removal of the marker. Audit rows the purge keeps name a tenant that no longer exists and are deleted by the audit retention sweep (T-119) like any other. Each completed purge is recorded in the system log as tenants.purged, beside the tenants.deleted record the deletion wrote, with the export time used and the audit rows deleted and kept; neither record is ever purged.

Every replica runs this sweep (#610). A tombstoned tenant is purged only by the replica whose SurrealTenantRepository::claim_purge wins it; the others skip it for TENANT_PURGE_LEASE. A tenant the purge finds already purged — gone before the claim, or removed by an overlapping purge after a lease ran out — is success, not a failure, and is not recorded again: only the purge that removed the tenant row writes tenants.purged. An orphaned id is claimed the same way, by a marker row (SurrealTenantRepository::claim_orphan_purge, R101W1-09 (a)): replicas whose daily scans coincide purge and record it once, and a scan that predates another replica’s finished purge finds nothing left, writes no marker and records nothing (#714).

Each tenant’s purge, from its claim to its record, is one unit of AuditedWork (#655): a task of its own, which aborting the cleanup task does not cut. The sweep starts no purge once the process is stopping (checked between tenants); a purge under way gives itself up at the stop as long as its tenant row is still there, releasing its claim, and once the row is removed it runs on to its record.

The removal leaves a tenant_purge_pending marker in its own transaction, which the unit clears once it has recorded the purge (#689). A unit cut in between leaves the marker, and every pass starts by recording what the markers left standing (record_pending_purges), so the next start writes the tenants.purged the cut unit did not, once.

  • Ok(n) is the number of tenants (and orphaned ids) this call purged.
  • One tenant’s failure does not stop the others; any failure makes the sweep fail, naming how many failed and never a tenant’s data. The failed tenant stays tombstoned, its claim is released, and the next pass resumes it (every step is idempotent).
  • A stop ends the pass early with Ok: the tenants left stay tombstoned and unclaimed, and the next start purges them.

§Errors

AxiamError::Internal as above, or the datastore error that kept the tombstoned tenants or the orphans from being listed (the tombstoned tenants are still purged when only the orphans could not be).
