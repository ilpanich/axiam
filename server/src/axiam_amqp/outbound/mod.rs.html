1//! The shared outbound dispatcher's AMQP machinery (D-36, T23.5.1).
2//!
3//! Everything here is parameterised by [`axiam_core::outbound::OutboundKind`];
4//! nothing here knows what a webhook, a Security Event Token or a SCIM
5//! operation is. The ports live in `axiam_core::outbound` (layer 0) and are
6//! documented there, including the three steps that add a kind.
7//!
8//! | Piece | Module |
9//! |---|---|
10//! | per-kind queue names and declaration arguments | [`topology`] |
11//! | bounded exponential retry policy and its env vars | [`retry`] |
12//! | the bytes on the queue | [`wire`] |
13//! | enqueue (implements `OutboundPublisher`) | [`publisher`] |
14//! | the outcome table: retry vs dead-letter vs delivered, and the audit row | `outcome` (crate-private) |
15//! | deliverer registry, the consume loop and its supervisor | [`consumer`] |
16//! | the in-process dispatcher of the minimal profile (no broker) | [`inprocess`] |
17//!
18//! The webhook kind keeps exactly the names, arguments, wire format and
19//! environment variables it had before the extraction. Renaming any of them
20//! would drop in-flight messages on upgrade or make the broker refuse a
21//! redeclaration; `topology::tests` pins them byte for byte.
22

24pub mod inprocess;

26pub mod publisher;
27pub mod retry;
28pub mod topology;
29pub mod wire;
